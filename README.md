## Overview of the manuscript

The manuscript presents a proposed proof of RH using an infinite-product representation of the completed Riemann zeta function, \(\xi(s)\). Its stated approach includes:

1. Pairing complex-conjugate zeros in the Hadamard product and grouping them with their multiplicities.
2. Expressing the resulting factors as irreducible real quadratic polynomials and establishing absolute and locally uniform convergence of the infinite product.
3. Applying the symmetric functional equation \(\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)\).
4. Using a divisibility property for entire functions, together with the uniqueness of zero multiplicities, to argue that the real parts of the zeros must equal \(1/2\).

These points summarize the strategy and conclusion stated by the manuscript; they are not an independent verification of the proof.
